FAQ
How do I pay with Heleket?

To pay using our payment system, you need to send the amount specified on the payment page to the wallet address indicated there.

Where can I get cryptocurrency for payment?

Please consider buying it on one of the convenient cryptocurrency exchanges like Binance, Coinbase, or Kraken.

What if I’ve selected the wrong network?

Unfortunately, it is not possible to change the network after it has been selected. To proceed, you need to create a new order on the store’s website and select the desired network during checkout.

Why has my transaction not been completed but the timer expired?

Your transaction has not yet received the necessary confirmations from the network. You don’t need to worry about the timer on the payment page.

Where can I check the status of my transaction?

Please track the status of the transaction in any Blockchain Explorer. Enter the transaction hash (TXID) to get all the necessary information.

My payment has not been credited, what should I do?

Different cryptocurrencies require a different number of confirmations. If the payment was not credited after receiving the required confirmations, please contact technical support.
